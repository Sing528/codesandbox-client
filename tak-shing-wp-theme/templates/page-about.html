About Tak Shing

For over 20 years, Tak Shing Environmental Management Company (達成環境管理服務公司) has provided trusted cleaning services throughout Hong Kong. Co-founded and led by Operating Manager Andy Lam (林文浩), our team is known for professionalism, safety, and dependable results.

We proudly serve homeowners, schools, offices, Michelin restaurants, public leisure venues, branded car dealerships, chain stores, government institutions, and industrial facilities. Our well-equipped teams are trained for specialized tasks such as marble restoration, nano disinfection spray (safe for humans and pets), post-construction cleaning, and high-altitude signage cleaning.
